Questions we hear most.

Which documents do I need?

Your GMP certificate, product registration, WHO prequalification where you have it, and a certificate of analysis. You share them once.

How are tenders priced?

Per unit, delivered. One quote reaches every buyer in the tender, under one contract.

Who reviews my documents?

Our QA team reviews them with you, and we reuse them on each tender.

When do I get paid?

Each order has clear milestones from purchase order to payment, and you can follow each one.

Do I need an office in each country?

No. Axmed pools buyers from many countries into one tender.
