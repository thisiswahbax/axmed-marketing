Questions we hear most.

What can I send?

The list you already have: Excel, PDF or a message. Sina matches each line to a real product.

How fast do I get an offer?

Within 48 hours of sending your list.

Do I need an account first?

No. Send your list and see the cost before you sign up.

Who checks quality?

Our QA and regulatory team checks the supplier, the product, its registration and the batch before anything ships.

Can you deliver to many sites?

Yes. One contract, delivered to the stores, hospitals and clinics on your list, and tracked to each one.

Is there help with funding?

Working capital can cover the gap while your funding lands, and the MNCH Matching Fund supports maternal and newborn health.
