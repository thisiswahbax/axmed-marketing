# Strata art

Layered terrain of many coloured strata settling into one landscape, after the "knowledge graph" art: many sources (buyers, suppliers, data) pooled into one shape. Dusty crests, vertical striation, film grain.

`python3 scripts/make-terrain.py` → stills in three palettes and three sizes. `python3 scripts/make-terrain.py --animate brand 1080x1350 6` → a slow looping MP4 (git-ignored; about 2 minutes to render).

| Palette | Use |
|---|---|
| `brand` | Indigo field with lilac, cyan and mist strata: hero sections, data stories, reports |
| `warm` | Adds sand and peach: impact and people stories, the annual report |
| `light` | Paper sky with pale strata: light pages, behind dark text in the sky area |

**Rules:** text sits in the sky (top) or on a flat panel below the art, never across the strata. One strata piece per page. Use it for "how it all comes together" ideas (data, pooled demand, the platform), not for product UI.
