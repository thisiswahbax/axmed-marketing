## Filter every reference through our rules

Keep: confident modern African settings, people at work with agency, clean data-led layouts,
glass/depth with restraint, bold type with lots of air, one accent colour.

Drop: poverty or pity imagery, NGO/humanitarian aesthetic, stock "doctor with stethoscope",
pills-and-capsules clichés, boxes-and-trucks only, neon "AI" clichés.

## Direction in one line

**Editorial, data-confident, human.** Big Instrument Sans headline, one real photo or one
platform moment, one cyan accent, generous indigo fields.
