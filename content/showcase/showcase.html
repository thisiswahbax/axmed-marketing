The Axmed marketing workspace

One repo where the team and Claude make on-brand campaigns, product imagery, motion and copy.

Showcase, 14 November 2026

Three layers, one repo.

Brand system

Logo, colours and type as tokens. The Sina orb. Photo, product-card, background, dot-matrix and strata systems. Hallmark anti-slop rules on everything.

Skills

Eight Axmed skills (posts, carousels, emails, WhatsApp cards, voice-over, video briefs, asset multiplier, brand check), plus Hallmark, marketing and Remotion skills and the ElevenLabs connection.

Strategy

Launch Playbook v7 as briefs and an asset plan, the fact sheet of approved claims, channels, measures, and what we may say about Sina and our AI.

How the team uses it.

  1. 1Ask in plain words.“Make the A1.5 carousel”, “Draft story email 1 for funders”, “Turn this interview into all the assets”.
  2. 2Claude reads the brand and the strategy.CLAUDE.md, the brand guidelines, the campaign brief and the fact sheet, before writing a word.
  3. 3Generators build the asset.HTML on brand/system.css, the orb, backgrounds, strata, dot-matrix, Remotion and ElevenLabs.
  4. 4Checks run before handover.Hallmark lint, the brand-reviewer agent, and every number checked against the fact sheet.
  5. 5You get files to post.PNG, PDF carousels, MP4 loops and copy, saved in content/ and pushed to GitHub.

Product cards, one tint per module.

Atmosphere backgrounds, one hue each.

Blurred tonal fields with film grain, after ElevenLabs. Always a stage for real content: a conversation, news, a statement.

10

Dot-matrix illustrations that move.

Dots scatter in, assemble, then a scan passes and one idea glints in cyan. Vial, ampoule, shield, box, globe, list, Sina, the mark. Export any shape as an MP4 loop.

11

Strata art for the big picture.

Layers settling into one landscape: data, demand and supply coming together. Stills and slow loops. Right: the LinkedIn video, “Medicines, delivered where they’re needed most.”

12

Line illustrations for how Axmed works.

Isometric wireframes, one indigo key line and one cyan point: pooled demand, one bundle, line by line, quality layers, every facility, Sina, planning 2027, the supply helix.
