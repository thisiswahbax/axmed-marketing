# Partner interview guide (20–30 min)

| # | Question | Listening for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell us about your work and the people you serve. | Scale, mission, human stakes |
| 2 | What were you trying to achieve, and what stood in the way? | Ambition and constraint, in their words |
| 3 | How did you approach it before? | The old way and its cost |
| 4 | Why did you try Axmed? | Trigger and decision criteria |
| 5 | What surprised you? | The quotable moment |
| 6 | What changed for your facilities, team or the people you serve? | Outcome, with numbers |
| 7 | What would you tell someone doing your job elsewhere? | The end-card line |

We never script a partner's praise. We ask, we listen, we use their words.
Record clean audio (lav mic) — the real voice carries the hero film. Transcribe with ElevenLabs
speech-to-text, then run `axmed-asset-multiplier` on the transcript.
