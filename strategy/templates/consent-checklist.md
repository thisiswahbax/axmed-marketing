# Consent and approval checklist

- [ ] Written consent from the partner organisation and every person filmed, photographed or quoted
- [ ] Scope agreed: channels (LinkedIn, website, email, paid) and duration
- [ ] Partner reviews the final cut and written case study before publication
- [ ] No patient-identifiable images without facility and individual consent; prefer facility, product and staff-at-work shots
- [ ] No partner footage/voice uploaded to AI tools that train on uploads (e.g. Higgsfield standard plan)
- [ ] No voice cloning or avatar of any partner, ever
