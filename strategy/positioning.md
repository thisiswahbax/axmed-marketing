# Positioning

## The engagement drive
A buyer sees our story on LinkedIn → gets a targeted procurement email a week later → meets us at
an event → gets a personal approach from GTM. Many campaigns, **one engagement drive**, every touch
pointing to the same offer.

**Campaign system:** DATA tells us whom to reach · STORIES give them a reason to believe · GTM gives
them a reason to act · BRAND makes Axmed the name they already know when GTM calls.

## Commercial hypothesis
Downstream (celebrate): procurers' work is complex and heroic — urgent hospital requests,
just-in-time logistics, many sites. Upstream (challenge): buying the products shouldn't be complex
but is — fragmented suppliers, slow tenders, opaque prices. Axmed makes the upstream simple.

## Proposition
Streamlined procurement as a genuine service: extensive QA, tailored execution, scale economics,
logistics excellence, delivery to multiple sites — powered by our technology (AI-driven quotes, AI
QA checks, 3D live order tracking, Match Fund co-financing).

## Audiences
| Audience | Who | What they must hear |
|---|---|---|
| Procurers (primary) | Governments, national medical stores, Nigerian state agencies, FBOs, NGOs, FP programmes | "They understand my work"; offer in 48 h; plan your 2027 procurement |
| Funders | Foundations funding procurement (e.g. CIFF) | Social mission, most cost-effective partner, technology of the future today |

## Trigger
Year end: replenishment orders now and 2027 budgeting. Every Nov–Jan asset carries a "plan your
2027 procurement" angle.

## CTA (in every asset)
Procurers: send an RFQ → offer within 48 hours · Funders: register an expression of interest ·
Everyone: activate a platform account. Act 2 invitation = **Benchmark Challenge**: "Send us your
2027 list. We'll show you how simple upstream can be."

## Example storyline (MNCH)
Healthcare workers do some of the most heroic work there is. The PPH bundle has changed how safe a
birth can be. But care starts long before the delivery room. Every life saved at birth starts with
a product that was there on time — the work of procurers. What shouldn't be complex, but today is,
is how they buy. That upstream is what Axmed champions. We make it simple, so procurers can focus
on the work only they can do, and health workers have what they need, every time.
