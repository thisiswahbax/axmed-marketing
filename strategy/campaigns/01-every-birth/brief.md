the PPH bundle is and why it works → the procurement officer who made sure it was there → "Care
starts long before the delivery room" → manifesto. *Measured by:* reach, followers, comments,
shares. Never judge it by RFQs.

**Act 2 — How it gets there, and join us.** LifeNet & RMS Rwanda: AI-driven quote → QA check →
Match Fund → 3D live tracking → multi-site delivery → "over 1 million drapes delivered" → invitation:
send your 2027 PPH bundle list or RFQ; offer within 48 hours. *Measured by:* target-account
engagement, RFQs, EOIs, meetings, opportunities.

Targeting: Market Development flags buyers with a fresh trigger (award, tender, published plan,
2027 budget) → they get the Act 2 invitation personally from GTM, not a broadcast.
