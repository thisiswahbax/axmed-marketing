# Axmed brand check

Read `brand/brand-guidelines.md` and `strategy/fact-sheet.md` first.

## Score the asset (pass / fix) on each line

1. **Hero** — a named person (midwife, procurement officer, programme lead) is the hero; Axmed is the enabler. Flag "revolutionising", "innovative platform", "we are proud to", Axmed-as-saviour.
2. **Status quo** — celebrates downstream work, challenges upstream constraint. Flag any blame of ministries/agencies/partners and any competitor name (IDA Foundation, Mission Pharma).
3. **Africa** — modern, confident, agency. Flag poverty/pity language, "beneficiaries", "the poor", "remote villages" framing, humanitarian tone.
4. **Proof** — every number matches the fact sheet's "Safe to say" wording exactly; evidence cited (E-MOTIVE / NEJM 2023 etc.). Flag "delivered" for non-fulfilled lines, "because of the bundle", unsourced savings, patient reach without methodology, "miracle drug" not attributed to a named health worker.
5. **Technology** — shows a concrete capability (AI quote, AI QA check, 3D live tracking, Match Fund). Flag buzzwords with nothing shown, or boxes-and-trucks-only framing.
6. **Bold & safe** — flag "disrupt", "hack", "move fast", shortcuts.
7. **FP only** — informed choice; flag volume/uptake targets as the story.
8. **AI guardrails** — flag any AI-generated person presented as real, synthetic testimonial, cloned/AI partner voice or avatar.
9. **CTA + 2027 angle** — CTA present (RFQ → 48 h offer / EOI / activate account) and a UTM link; Nov–Jan assets carry "plan your 2027 procurement".
10. **Voice** — short sentences, active, British spelling, ≤3 hashtags, no emoji on the company page.

## Then apply the central test
"Does this make the buyer feel seen, respected and more ambitious about what they can achieve, without compromise? And does it show Axmed empowers them with unmatched technology?" Answer yes/no with one sentence why.

## Output
A table of the 10 checks (✅ / ⚠️ fix + quoted offending text), the central-test verdict, then a **rewritten version** that fixes every ⚠️ without changing approved facts. Unknown numbers → `[NUMBER NEEDED: …]`.
